(02) — CHRONICLE / 1994 TO TODAY

The thread of three decades.

An unbroken commitment to ring-spun yarn uniformity, generational local artisans, and absolute dispatch honesty in Tamil Nadu’s textile core.

1994

The Founding Shed

LOT 001 · 4 CIRCULAR FRAMES

Four second-hand circular knitting machines in a 1,200 sq.ft shed in Angeripalayam. Our founder, R. Natarajan, sets a rule that never broke: only pure combed ring-spun cotton, zero synthetic blends unless explicitly contracted by tech-pack.

FOUNDER: R. NATARAJAN KNITTING SPEED: 18 RPM
