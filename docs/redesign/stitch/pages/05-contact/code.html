(04) — SOURCING FAQ & TERMS OF BUSINESS

Commercial procurement protocols.

01 / MOQ SLABS

What is the absolute minimum order?

For stocked premium blanks (180/240 GSM single jersey, hoodies, piqué polos), our MOQ is 50 pieces per color/size run. For custom dyeing to exact Pantone standards, our vat dyeing MOQ is 200 pieces.

02 / LAB DIPS & SWATCHES

How fast do fabric lab-dips ship?

Custom shade matching (A/B/C options on your specified GSM) takes 3 to 5 business days from receipt of your physical sample or Pantone TCX code. Physical swatch hangers ship worldwide via DHL Express.

03 / PAYMENT & FREIGHT

Payment terms & Export FOB terms?

Domestic deliveries require 50% advance upon cutting authorization and balance against dispatch LR. Export deliveries operate on verified Letter of Credit (LC at Sight) or Wire Transfer with FOB Tuticorin or CIF options.
